Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here is how Spendly handles your information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, and the expense details you log, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type, to keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to provide and improve Spendly: to let you sign in, record and review your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is accessible only to you through your account. We take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorized access, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can delete your expenses or your account at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a limited number of third party services, such as Google Fonts for typography. These services may receive technical information like your IP address when your browser loads them. We do not share your expense data with any third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please get in touch with us through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
